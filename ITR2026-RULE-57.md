## 3. ⭐⭐ The list still does not reach a virtual digital asset

Column C enumerates **jewellery · artistic work · quoted shares · unquoted shares · one further entry.** Five categories.

**The 2026 Rules were notified on 20 March 2026 — four years after virtual digital assets entered the Act. The drafter rewrote this rule from scratch and still did not add them.**
