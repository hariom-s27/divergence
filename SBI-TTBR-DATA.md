## ⭐ THE FOUR-DAY HOLE

SBI published on Thursday the 25th, **skipped Friday the 26th**, and did not publish again until Monday the 29th.

**A payment settling at 03:14 IST on Sunday 28 June 2026 has no mandated rate in existence.**

## The second undetermined choice — the gap inside the gap

Even doing the legally correct thing, you must choose:
- Last published **before**: 25 June, ₹94.00 → ₹4,70,517 on $5,000
- Next published **after**: 29 June, ₹93.95 → ₹4,69,750

**Difference ₹250. Nothing prescribes which.**

## Two practical notes
- Only rates published for the ₹10–20 lakh transaction band are reference rates. They do not vary by transaction size.
- Where SBI does not publish, the common practitioner workaround is to take the RBI reference rate and adjust by a "standard delta" of 20–30 paise. **That workaround has no statutory basis whatsoever — a fourth undetermined choice.**
