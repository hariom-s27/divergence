# Section 439(8) — what does NOT count as under-reported income

**(a)** the amount of income in respect of which the assessee offers an explanation and the Competent Authority is satisfied that the explanation is bona fide and the assessee has disclosed all the material facts to substantiate the explanation offered;

**(b)** the amount of under-reported income determined on the basis of an estimate, if the accounts are correct and complete to the satisfaction of the Competent Authority but the method employed is such that the income cannot properly be deduced therefrom;

## ⚠️ OUR LIMB IS (a), NOT (b)

Limb **(b)** concerns the *Assessing Officer* estimating because the taxpayer's accounting method does not allow income to be deduced. **That is not our situation.**

**Limb (a)** — bona fide explanation with all material facts disclosed — is ours, and it is where the case law sits:

> Penalty is *"discretionary, not automatic, and cannot be levied where the addition is based on a difference in legal view or **computational methodology** rather than suppression of income."*

> *"A bona fide, fully disclosed, debatable tax position cannot be penalised merely because the Revenue disagrees."*

**A valuation-method disagreement is a computational-methodology difference — in a tribunal's own words.**
