# Rule 115 — Rate of exchange for conversion into rupees of income expressed in foreign currency
<!-- VERBATIM-START -->

**115. (1)** *The rate of exchange for the calculation of the value in rupees of any income accruing or arising or deemed to accrue or arise to the assessee **in foreign currency** or received or deemed to be received by him or on his behalf **in foreign currency** shall be the telegraphic transfer buying rate of such currency as on the specified date.*

*Explanation.—For the purposes of this rule,—*

*(1) "telegraphic transfer buying rate" shall have the same meaning as in the Explanation to rule 26;*

*(2) "specified date" means—*
*(a) in respect of income chargeable under the head "Salaries", the last day of the month immediately preceding the month in which the salary is due, or is paid in advance or in arrears;*
*(b) in respect of income by way of "interest on securities", the last day of the month immediately preceding the month in which the income is due;*
***(c) in respect of income chargeable under the heads "Income from house property", "Profits and gains of business or profession" [not being income referred to in clause (d)] and "Income from other sources" (not being income by way of dividends and "Interest on securities"), the last day of the previous year of the assessee;***
*(d) in respect of income chargeable under the head "Profits and gains of business or profession" in the case of a non-resident engaged in the business of operation of ships, the last day of the month immediately preceding the month in which such income is deemed to accrue or arise in India;*
*(e) in respect of income by way of dividends, the last day of the month immediately preceding the month in which the dividend is declared, distributed or paid by the company;*
*(f) in respect of income chargeable under the head "Capital gains", the last day of the month immediately preceding the month in which the capital asset is transferred:*
*Provided that the specified date, in respect of income referred to in sub-clauses (a) to (f) payable in foreign currency and from which tax has been deducted at source under rule 26, shall be the date on which the tax was required to be deducted under the provisions of the Chapter XVII-B.*

**(2)** *Nothing contained in sub-rule (1) shall apply in respect of income referred to in clause (c) of the Explanation to sub-rule (1) where such income is received in, or brought into India by the assessee or on his behalf before the specified date in accordance with the provisions of the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1973.*

# ⛔⛔ WE HAD THIS WRONG. RULE 115 DOES NOT APPLY.

For several days we have described the SBI TT buying rate as **"the rate Rule 115 mandates"** and used it as Method B. **That is incorrect, and the correction makes the thesis stronger.**

## Read the opening words

> *"...the value in rupees of any income accruing or arising ... **in foreign currency** or received ... **in foreign currency**..."*

**Rule 115 converts income received in FOREIGN CURRENCY. Nothing else.**

And **s.2(47A)** of the Income-tax Act defines a virtual digital asset as *"any information or code or number or token **(not being Indian currency or foreign currency)**"* — expressly adopting FEMA's definitions.

> ## **The Act says a VDA is not foreign currency. Rule 115 only converts foreign currency. Therefore Rule 115 does not reach a USDC receipt at all.**

**This is not an interpretation. It is the two texts read side by side.**

## And even if it did apply, it would not give a settlement-date rate

Explanation (2)(c): for **"Profits and gains of business or profession"**, the specified date is **the last day of the previous year.**

So for our 29 June 2026 receipt it would take the rate on **31 March 2027** — nine months later, and nothing to do with the moment the money arrived.

## Four provisions. None reaches.

| Provision | Why it does not answer |
|---|---|
| **Rule 11UA / 57** — fair market value | Scoped to s.56; enumerates jewellery, art, shares. Zero VDA references |
| **s.2(22B)** — fair market value | Scoped to *a capital asset*; points at rules that do not cover VDAs |
| **s.115BBH** — 30% on VDA transfer | Contains the word "value" **zero** times |
| **Rule 115** — rate of exchange | **Converts foreign currency. A VDA is defined as not being foreign currency** |

**Four separate doors. All four locked, each for a different reason.** That is not an oversight — it is a pattern.

## 🔴 Correct the language everywhere

❌ *"Method B — the rate Rule 115 mandates"*
✅ **"Method B — the SBI telegraphic transfer buying rate, which is what Rule 115 would require if Rule 115 applied. By the Act's own definition, it does not."**

**Longer, and true.** And it turns Method B from a competing answer into **a second demonstration that nothing governs.**
