# FEMA 1999 — Sections 3, 7 and 8

## ⭐⭐ Section 3 — THE PROVISION WE HAD NEVER LOOKED AT

<!-- VERBATIM-START -->
**3. Dealing in foreign exchange, etc.—** *Save as otherwise provided in this Act, rules or regulations made thereunder, or with the general or special permission of the Reserve Bank, no person shall—*

*(a) deal in or transfer any foreign exchange or foreign security to any person not being an authorised person;*

*(b) **make any payment to or for the credit of any person resident outside India in any manner**;*

*(c) **receive otherwise through an authorised person, any payment by order or on behalf of any person resident outside India in any manner**;*

*Explanation.—For the purpose of this clause, where any person in, or resident in, India receives any payment by order or on behalf of any person resident outside India through any other person (including an authorised person) **without a corresponding inward remittance from any place outside India**, then, such person shall be deemed to have received such payment otherwise than through an authorised person;*

*(d) enter into any financial transaction in India as consideration for or in association with acquisition or creation or transfer of a right to acquire, any asset outside India by any person.*

<!-- VERBATIM-END -->
## Section 7 — Export of goods and services (COMPLETE)

<!-- VERBATIM-START -->
**7. (1)** *Every exporter of goods shall—*
*(a) furnish to the Reserve Bank or to such other authority a declaration in such form and in such manner as may be specified, containing true and correct material particulars, including the amount representing the full export value or, if the full export value of the goods is not ascertainable at the time of export, the value which the exporter, having regard to the prevailing market conditions, expects to receive on the sale of the goods in a market outside India;*
*(b) furnish to the Reserve Bank such other information as may be required by the Reserve Bank for the purpose of ensuring the realisation of the export proceeds by such exporter.*

**(2)** *The Reserve Bank may, for the purpose of ensuring that the full export value of the goods or such reduced value of the goods as the Reserve Bank determines, having regard to the prevailing market conditions, is received without any delay, direct any exporter to comply with such requirements as it deems fit.*

**(3)** ***Every exporter of services shall furnish to the Reserve Bank or to such other authorities a declaration in such form and in such manner as may be specified, containing the true and correct material particulars in relation to payment for such services.***

<!-- VERBATIM-END -->
## Section 8 — Realisation and repatriation (COMPLETE)

<!-- VERBATIM-START -->
**8.** *Save as otherwise provided in this Act, where any amount of **foreign exchange** is due or has accrued to any person resident in India, such person shall take all reasonable steps to realise and repatriate to India such foreign exchange within such period and in such manner as may be specified by the Reserve Bank.*

# ⭐⭐ SECTION 3(c) CHANGES THE FEMA CLAIM ENTIRELY

**We have spent fifteen steps carefully hedging the FEMA position as "an inference from s.2(n) read with ss.7–8." We were looking at the wrong sections.**

**Section 3(c) is an express prohibition:**

> *"no person shall … receive otherwise [than] through an authorised person, any payment by order or on behalf of any person resident outside India in any manner"*

**Apply it to our facts, term by term:**

| Element of s.3(c) | Our freelancer |
|---|---|
| a person | ✅ resident individual |
| receives a payment | ✅ 5,000 USDC as consideration for services |
| by order or on behalf of a person resident outside India | ✅ the US client |
| otherwise than through an authorised person | ✅ **no bank was involved at any point** |

**And the Explanation closes the remaining door:** receipt *"without a corresponding inward remittance from any place outside India"* is **deemed** to be receipt otherwise than through an authorised person.

## Note what s.3(c) does NOT say

**It does not say "foreign exchange." It says "any payment … in any manner."**

Compare s.3(a), which is limited to *"any foreign exchange or foreign security"*, and s.8, which bites only on *"foreign exchange."* **The drafter used the narrow term where he meant it and the broad term where he did not.**

**So the argument that a stablecoin is not foreign exchange — which defeats s.8 — does not defeat s.3(c).** The breadth of "any payment … in any manner" is doing deliberate work.

## What is now express and what remains open

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| A VDA is not foreign currency or foreign exchange | ✅ **Express** — s.2(47A) IT Act adopting FEMA s.2(h)(m)(q) |
| **Receiving a payment from a person resident outside India otherwise than through an authorised person is prohibited** | ✅ **EXPRESS — s.3(c)** |
| An exporter of services must declare the particulars of payment | ✅ Express — s.7(3) |
| Foreign exchange due must be realised and repatriated | ✅ Express — s.8 (but bites on foreign exchange, which she never received) |
| **Is a stablecoin transfer a "payment" for s.3(c)?** | ⚠️ **The only question left** |

**That last row is the whole remaining inference, and it is a much narrower one than we have been carrying.**

## 🔴 REWRITE THE FEMA LANGUAGE EVERYWHERE

❌ *"Very likely non-compliant by inference from s.2(n) read with ss.7–8."*

✅ **"Section 3(c) prohibits receiving any payment, in any manner, on behalf of a person resident outside India otherwise than through an authorised person — and deems receipt without a corresponding inward remittance to be exactly that. No bank was involved here. The only remaining question is whether a stablecoin transfer is a 'payment', and nothing in the section suggests it is not."**

**Shorter. Sharper. And it names a prohibition rather than an inference.**

## Section 9 — check the exemptions before finalising
s.9 exempts certain cases from ss.4 and 8 — including *"foreign exchange acquired from employment, business, trade, vocation, services… up to such limit as the Reserve Bank may specify."*

**Note: s.9 exempts from ss.4 and 8. It does not exempt from s.3.** Worth stating.
