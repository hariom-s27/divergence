# Rule 206 — Rate of exchange for conversion into rupees of income expressed in foreign currency

## ✅ U12 CLOSED. Rule 115 (1962) → Rule 206 (2026), confirmed from the CBDT Navigator.

<!-- VERBATIM-START -->

**206. Rate of exchange for conversion into rupees of income expressed in foreign currency.–**

**(1)** *The rate of exchange for the calculation of the value in rupees of any income, accruing or arising or deemed to accrue or arise to the assessee **in foreign currency**, or received or deemed to be received by him or on his behalf **in foreign currency**, shall be the telegraphic transfer buying rate of such currency as on the specified date.*

**(2)** *For the purposes of this rule—*

*(a) **"telegraphic transfer buying rate" shall have the meaning assigned to it in rule 207**; and*

*(b) (i) the "specified date" in respect of the nature of income referred in column B of the following Table shall be as given in column C:*

**Table**

| Sl. No — A | Types of Income — B | **Specified Date — C** |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Income chargeable under the head "Salaries".* | *Last day of the month, immediately preceding the month in which salary is due, or is paid in advance, or arrears.* |
| 2 | *Income by way of interest on securities.* | *Last day of the month, immediately preceding the month in which the income is due.* |
| **3** | ***Income chargeable under the heads "Income from house property", "Profits and gains of business or profession" (not being income referred to in Sl.No. 4), and "Income from other sources" (excluding dividends and interest on securities).*** | ***Last day of the tax year of the assessee.*** |
| 4 | *Income chargeable under the head "Profits and gains of business or profession" in the case of a non-resident engaged in the business of operation of ships.* | *Last day of the month, immediately preceding the month in which the income is deemed to arise in India.* |
| 5 | *Income by way of dividends.* | *Last day of the month, immediately preceding the month in which dividend is declared, distributed, or paid by the company.* |
| 6 | *Income chargeable under the head "Capital gains".* | *Last day of the month, immediately preceding the month of in which the capital asset in the table is transferred;* |

*(ii) the specified date, in respect of income referred to in sub-clause (ii), payable in foreign currency and from which tax has been deducted at source under rule 207, shall be the date on which the tax was required to be deducted under the provisions of the Chapter XIX-B.*

**(3)** *Nothing contained in sub-rule (1) shall apply in respect of income referred to in sub-rule (2)(b) [Table: Sl.No. 3], where such income is received in, or brought into India by the assessee or on his behalf before the specified date, in accordance with the provisions of the **Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1973 (46 of 1973)**.*

# ⛔ RULE 206 DOES NOT REACH A VDA RECEIPT — TWICE OVER

## (a) It converts **foreign currency**, and a VDA is defined as not being one

Sub-rule (1) opens: *"any income … **in foreign currency**, or received or deemed to be received by him or on his behalf **in foreign currency**."*

**Section 2(111)** [formerly s.2(47A)] defines a virtual digital asset as *"any information or code or number or token **(not being Indian currency or foreign currency)**"* — and its Explanation expressly adopts the meanings of "currency", "foreign currency" and "Indian currency" from **clauses (h), (m) and (q) of section 2 of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999.**

> **The Act says a VDA is not foreign currency. Rule 206 converts only foreign currency. It does not reach a USDC receipt at all.**

**This is not an interpretation. It is two texts read side by side.**

## (b) And even if it did apply, it would not give a settlement-date rate

Table Sl. No. 3 covers *"Profits and gains of business or profession"* — our freelancer's head of income. Its specified date is **"Last day of the tax year of the assessee."**

For a 28 June 2026 receipt that is **31 March 2027** — nine months later, and nothing to do with the moment the money arrived.

## 🔴 Correct the language everywhere

❌ *"Rule 115 mandates the SBI TT buying rate"*
✅ **"Rule 206 (formerly Rule 115) would require the SBI telegraphic transfer buying rate if it applied. By the Act's own definition of a virtual digital asset, it does not — and if it did, it would point at 31 March, not at the date of receipt."**

Longer, and true. It turns Method B from a competing answer into **a second demonstration that nothing governs.**

# ⭐⭐ THE ASYMMETRY — RULE 206 BORROWS THE DEFINITION AND LEAVES THE REMEDY

**Rule 206(2)(a):** *"'telegraphic transfer buying rate' shall have the meaning assigned to it in **rule 207**."*

**Rule 207(1)**, the sentence being borrowed from:

> *"…shall be the telegraphic transfer buying rate of such currency as on the date on which such tax is required to be deducted…; **but where the telegraphic transfer buying rate is not published on such date, the last such published rate may be taken.**"*

|  | Paying **out** to a non-resident — Rule 207 | **Receiving** as a resident — Rule 206 |
|---|---|---|
| Defines "telegraphic transfer buying rate" | ✅ r.207(3)(b) | borrows it, by express cross-reference, from r.207 |
| **Rate not published on the required date?** | ✅ **"the last such published rate may be taken"** | ❌ **nothing** |

> ## **Rule 206 reaches into Rule 207 for the meaning of the term. The fallback for when that rate does not exist sits in the same sentence as the term being borrowed. Rule 206 takes the definition across and leaves the remedy behind.**

The drafter's eye was on that exact sentence — he cited it. He carried the term and not the sentence's second half.

**Two independent instances of the same asymmetry** (this, and Rule 57's column B) **is a pattern, not an oversight.** One looks like an accident. Two looks like the shape of the instrument.

See `ITR2026-RULE-207.md`.

# ⭐⭐⭐ SUB-RULE (3) CITES AN ACT REPEALED TWENTY-SIX YEARS AGO

Rule 206(3), notified **20 March 2026**, reads:

> *"…in accordance with the provisions of the **Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1973 (46 of 1973)**."*

**FERA 1973 was repealed by the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, with effect from 1 June 2000.**

This is not a drafting habit across the instrument. Machine-counted across the entire notified Rules 2026:

```
"Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1973"  ....... 1 occurrence  — Rule 206(3)
"Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999"  ....... 3 occurrences — including Rule 210
```

**Rule 206 cites the repealed Act. Rule 210 — four rules later, on the same page range — cites the current one.**

> ## **In a rulebook notified on 20 March 2026, four rules apart, the drafter cited the Act in force in one rule and an Act repealed in 2000 in the other. The stale one is the rule that governs converting our freelancer's income into rupees.**
