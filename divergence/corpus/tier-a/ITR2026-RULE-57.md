# Rule 57, Income-tax Rules, 2026 — Determination of fair market value

## ✅ READ FROM THE GAZETTE PAGE TEXT, 18 AUGUST 2026. Seven rows, not five.

<!-- VERBATIM-START -->

**57. Determination of fair market value.–** *For the purpose of following sections referred to in column B of the Table below, the fair market value of the property of the nature referred to column C shall be determined in the manner provided in column D thereof:*

**Table**

| Sl. No — A | **Section — B** | **Nature of Property — C** |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Sections 26(2)(j) and 92.* | *Jewellery* |
| 2 | *Sections 26(2)(j) and 92.* | *Archaeological collections, drawings, paintings, sculptures or any work of art (herein referred as artistic work)* |
| 3 | *Sections 26(2)(j) and 92.* | *Quoted shares and securities* |
| 4 | *Sections 26(2)(j), 72 and 92.* | *Unquoted equity shares* |
| 5 | *Sections 26(2)(j), 72 and 92.* | *Unquoted shares and securities (other than equity shares in a company) which are not listed in any recognised stock exchange* |
| **6** | ***Section 26(2)(j)*** | *Immovable property being land or building or both* |
| **7** | ***Section 26(2)(j)*** | ***Any other property other than referred to at Sl. Nos. 1 to 6 above.*** |

**Column D, row 6:** *"The value adopted or assessed or assessable by any authority of the Central Government or a State Government for the purpose of payment of stamp duty in the respect of such immovable property on the valuation date."*

**Column D, row 7:** *"The price that such property would ordinarily fetch on sale in the open market on the valuation date."*

<!-- VERBATIM-END -->

---

# ⭐ THE STRONGEST COUNTER-ARGUMENT TO THIS PROJECT — AND ITS ANSWER

## The attack

**Row 7 is a residual catch-all with an open-market fallback.** *"Any other property other than referred to at Sl. Nos. 1 to 6 above"* → *"the price that such property would ordinarily fetch on sale in the open market on the valuation date."*

A virtual digital asset is "any other property." So the rule **does** reach it, there **is** a prescribed method, and the whole project collapses.

**State this attack out loud before anyone else does.** It is real, it is the first thing a competent reader will find, and answering it from the page is worth more than never raising it.

## The answer, from column B

| Rows | Column B, exact text |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | `Sections 26(2)(j) and 92.` |
| 4–5 | `Sections 26(2)(j), 72 and 92.` |
| **6–7** | **`Section 26(2)(j)`** |

**Section 92 is not in column B for rows 6 or 7.**

Section 92 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 is the successor to s.56(2)(x) — the receipt-without-consideration provision, and the provision through which "property" was extended by name to include a virtual digital asset.

> ## **The catch-all that would have caught a VDA is, by the drafter's own column B, unavailable to section 92.**
>
> **This is not an argument from silence. It is a drafting choice visible on the page.**

## Why the scoping is deliberate, not accidental

The **CBDT Navigator** (`navigatorIncometaxRules2026.pdf`, row 57) maps:

> **Rule 57 (2026) ← Rules 11UA / 11UAA / 11UAB (1962)**

Three rules consolidated into one table.

- **Rows 1–5** descend from **Rule 11UA**, which opened *"For the purposes of section 56 of the Act…"* → s.92
- **Rows 6–7** descend from **Rule 11UAB**, which was always scoped to **s.28(via)** — inventory converted into a capital asset → **s.26(2)(j)**

**Rule 11UA had no residual clause at all.** The catch-all entered this table from 11UAB, carrying 11UAB's scope with it. The drafter consolidated three rules and preserved each one's reach in column B.

**Two independent confirmations of the s.56(2)(x) → s.92 mapping**, from the same Navigator:
- **Rule 58 ← Rules 11UAC / 11UAD** — *"Prescribed class of persons for the purpose of section 92(3)(i) and section 79"*. 11UAC/11UAD were the s.56(2)(x) exclusion rules.
- **Rule 59 ← Rule 11UACA** — *"Computation of income chargeable to tax under **section 92(2)(l)**"*. 11UACA was the s.56(2)(xiii) computation rule.

# ⭐⭐ THE RULES PRESCRIBE THE DATE AND REFUSE TO PRESCRIBE THE METHOD

**Rule 56(i)** — the definitions rule immediately preceding this one — fixes the valuation date:

| Sl. | Section | Valuation date |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Section 92** | **"Date on which property or consideration, as the case may be, referred to in section 92 is received by the assessee."** |

> **For a section 92 receipt, the Rules tell you exactly *when* to value: the moment it is received. Four lines later, Rule 57's table does not tell you *how*.**

The gap is not vagueness about timing. **The timing is prescribed to the day and the method is absent.** See `ITR2026-RULE-56.md`.
