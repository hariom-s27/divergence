## The "no prescribed method" claim
This is a **negative** claim, and a negative cannot be evidenced by a positive primary source. So the support is professional commentary, and we say so.

- Majmudar & Partners / Simmons & Simmons: *"Currently, there is no valuation method prescribed under the IT Act for determining the FMV of VDAs."*
- Rule 11UA/57 prescribes FMV for immovable property, jewellery and shares. Its amendments (Notification 61/2017, 81/2023) concern shares and CCPS only.
- CBDT VDA notifications 74/2022 and 75/2022 only **exclude** items from the VDA definition. Neither prescribes valuation.

## Negative evidence from the guides
Across every practitioner guide reviewed — CoinDCX, ClearTax, Cointelegraph, CAclubindia, KoinX — **not one names a rate source.** They all cover the 30%, the 1% TDS, thresholds and Schedule VDA, and skip the single most practical question a reader has.

**If a settled convention existed, these guides would state it.** Real evidence, but weak — absence in guides is not absence in practice. Only a practitioner closes this.
